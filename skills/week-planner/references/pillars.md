# Content pillars

Pick 3 or 4 per week. Each pillar answers "why would my audience care?"

| Pillar | What it is | Good for | Example hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teach | A tip, how-to, mistake to avoid | Trust, saves, shares | "Three things I check before..." |
| Behind the scenes | Process, workspace, people, prep | Connection | "What a Tuesday here looks like" |
| Proof | Real results, real customer words, before and after | Sales | Only with real, permitted quotes |
| Story | Why you started, a lesson, a failure | Connection, followers | "The order that almost made me quit" |
| Offer | Product, booking, event, launch | Sales | "Booking opens Thursday" (true dates only) |
| Answer | A question people keep asking (from the inbox) | Trust, fewer repeat DMs | "You asked: do we deliver on Fridays?" |
| Community | Reposts, shout-outs, local news, questions to the audience | Comments | "Which one should we make next?" |

## Matching pillars to goals

- **Sales or bookings**: Offer, Proof, Answer, plus one Teach.
- **Followers**: Teach, Story, Community.
- **Trust for a new business**: Behind the scenes, Teach, Answer.

## Reuse the inbox

Repeat questions from `inbox-triage` make the best Answer posts: real demand, the user's own words, and fewer DMs next week.
